Questions, answered

Short answers. Longer ones live on the blog.

What are the most common Spanish words?

Function words and core verbs: ser, estar, ir, tener, haber, qué, hacer, poder. They appear in nearly every sentence, which is why the top 100 cover about half of everyday Spanish.

How many words do I need to be conversational?

Around 500. That covers roughly 67% of everyday Spanish, enough for basic back-and-forth about your day, work and family.

What’s the difference between the free tools and the workbooks?

The free tools are for studying on screen. The workbooks are printable PDFs with a fixed practice method, for people who learn better with a pen.

Do the workbooks overlap?

Yes. Each one starts from word #1, so the Top 1,000 includes the Top 500. Pick the level you want to reach; you don’t need to buy them in sequence.

How long does it take to learn 1,500 words?

With 15–20 minutes a day, most adults get there in four to eight months. The first 100 take one to two weeks.

Do I need an account?

No. Flashcards and the level test run in your browser. Workbooks are a one-time download, delivered to your email.
