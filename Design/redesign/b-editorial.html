01 — Coverage

How many words do you actually need?

Fewer than you think. Spanish leans on a small core of words. Each row below is how much of everyday Spanish you recognise once you know that many, in frequency order.

05 — Questions

Short answers.

How many Spanish words do I need to be conversational?
About 500. That covers roughly 67% of everyday Spanish, enough to talk about your day, work and family.
Are the workbooks physical books?
No. They're PDFs you download right after checkout and print at home or at a copy shop. A4 and US Letter are both included.
Which workbook should I buy first?
The Top 500, unless the level test puts you past rank 500. Then start with the Top 1,000.
Is the online list really free?
Yes. The full list, the level test and the first 100 flashcards need no account. The rest of the flashcards unlock with your email.
Why learn by frequency instead of by topic?
Topic lists spend your time on words you'll rarely meet. Frequency order means every word you learn is one you'll hear this week.
How long does the Top 500 take?
At one unit a day, 50 days. Go faster or slower; the review boxes keep track either way.
