Questions

How many Spanish words do I need to understand everyday conversation?

About 1,500 high-frequency words gets you to roughly 80% of everyday spoken Spanish. 500 is enough for basic conversation; that already covers about 67%.

Are the flashcards and level test really free?

Yes. The first 100 flashcards, the level test and every word list are free, with no account. Your progress stays in your browser.

What exactly is in a workbook?

A printable PDF: 10 units of 50 ranked words, exercises built from real example sentences, a review calendar, an answer key and a mastery tracker. Print at home or work on a tablet.

Which workbook should I start with?

Top 500, unless the level test says you already know most of those words. Each workbook starts where the previous one ends.

Do the workbooks repeat the free flashcards?

They use the same ranked list, so they line up. The flashcards train recognition; the workbooks add written recall and production, which is where words stick.
