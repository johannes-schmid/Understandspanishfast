Every unit, 4 pages

  • Pp 1–2, word entries. Rank, word, part of speech, meaning, the real example sentence with translation, a write-it line and four review tick-boxes.
  • P 3, practice. Fill the gap from the unit's own sentences, plus a 6-item match.
  • P 4, use it. Translate into Spanish, a 10-gap story cloze that recycles earlier units, and the unit checkpoint (18/20 to move on).

Top 1,000, 1,500, 2,000

  • Next band only. Each covers 500 new ranks (501–1,000, 1,001–1,500, 1,501–2,000) and assumes the bands before it.
  • Lighter per word. 25 words per unit on one page of entries; practice shifts from single sentences to longer stories, since the reader already has the core 500.
  • Mixed-band tests. Cumulative tests pull 20% of items from earlier books, so old words stay alive. About 110–120 pages each.

Data gap for the Top 2,000. data/words.json holds 1,500 ranked words today. The 2,000 book needs ranks 1,501–2,000 added, with meanings, POS and example sentences, before it can be typeset.

The mastery methodPart I

20 words a day.
Five looks at each.

You do not need willpower. You need the right words, in order, seen again just before you forget them. Every unit in this book is 20 words. You learn one unit a day and review earlier units on a fixed schedule.

Day 0Learn

Read each word aloud, copy it once, cover the meaning and recall it.

+1 · +3Recall

Cover the English column. Tick each word you know in under 3 seconds.

+7 · +14Use

Redo the unit's cloze story without looking at the word list.
